Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Daily Buddy ("the app", "we") handles information. We do not collect your personal information. The app has no accounts, and everything you log stays on your device. During the free trial the app shows non-personalized banner ads served by Google AdMob (see below).

Data stored on your device

Your logs (such as farts, pee, poop, water, medicines and custom trackers), pets and pet photos, medicine names, notes, reminder time and settings are stored only on your iPhone, protected by iOS data protection, and in your device backups. They are never sent to us or to any third party. You can export them as PDF or Excel at any time, and delete everything with Settings > Privacy & Data > Delete All Data. Deleting the app also deletes this data from the device.

Apple Health

If you choose to connect Apple Health, the app reads only the data types you allow (such as steps, activity, sleep and vitals) to show them on your device. Health data is never stored by the app, never included in exports, never sent anywhere, and never used for advertising or marketing. The app does not write to Apple Health. You can turn this off in the app or in the Health app at any time.

Photos

If you add your own picture for a pet or tracker, you choose it with Apple's photo picker. The app receives only that one picture and keeps it on your device. It has no access to the rest of your photo library.

Advertising during the free trial

Banner ads are shown only during the 7-day free trial and removed once your paid subscription starts. Ads are requested as non-personalized, and the app does not ask to track you across other apps and websites. To show and measure ads, Google may collect device identifiers, IP address-based approximate location, information about the ads you see and interact with, and diagnostic data such as crash logs and performance data, as described in Google's advertising policies and Privacy Policy. Your logs, notes, photos and Health data are never shared with Google or any ad network. We do not use analytics tools.

Consent choices

Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland) you'll be asked for consent before ads use your data, and you can change it any time with Ad Privacy Choices in Settings > Privacy & Data. The Consent & Data page in the app lists each permission, its purpose and when you last changed it.

Notifications and App Lock

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app asks permission to send notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your device and never mention what you track. If you turn on App Lock, Face ID or Touch ID is handled by iOS; the app never receives your biometric data. An optional Daily Buddy passcode is stored only as a secure hash in your device's keychain.

Subscriptions and payments

Purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details. Apple tells the app only whether your trial or subscription is active. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Contacting support

If you email us, we receive your email address and message and use them only to reply.

Children's privacy

The app is intended for teens and adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it was last changed.
