Daily Buddy

A cute, private daily tracker. Log farts, pee, poop, water and medicines in one tap, feed your pets, and see your day at a glance.

Features

  • One-tap Fart, Pee, Poop, Water and Medicines logging, with − to fix a mistake
  • Water in glasses, cups or bottles (small, medium, large), mL or fl oz
  • Medicines and pets, each with their own names and kawaii icons or your own photos
  • Custom trackers, daily notes, a shareable Your Day recap and day/week/month/year history
  • Optional Apple Health view (read only), daily reminder, Face ID / Touch ID / passcode lock
  • PDF and Excel export; no account, everything stays on your iPhone

Free trial and subscription

New subscribers get a 7-day free trial, then a monthly subscription (CAD $1.99 or USD $1.99, subject to App Store pricing in your region). Small banner ads show during the trial only; once your subscription starts the app is ad-free. Cancel anytime in your Apple Account settings.

Daily Buddy is for personal tracking and general information only. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. If you have a health concern, contact your doctor or qualified health professional.
