Support

Need help with Daily Buddy? Email us at chowchowbuy@gmail.com and we'll get back to you.

How does the free trial work?

The 7-day free trial starts when you confirm it in the app. Unless you cancel at least 24 hours before it ends, it becomes a monthly subscription charged to your Apple Account. Ads stop once the paid subscription starts.

How do I cancel?

Open the iOS Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Daily Buddy. Deleting the app does not cancel your subscription.

I subscribed on another device. How do I get access?

Sign in with the same Apple Account and tap Restore Purchases in Settings > Subscription or on the subscription screen.

I logged something by mistake.

Tap − next to the + to remove the latest entry from today, or open the item to edit or delete any entry and change its time.

How do I add medicines or pets?

Tap the + on the Medicines or Pets card on Home, or open the Meds & Pets tab. Each one can have its own name, type, icon or photo.

Apple Health shows nothing.

Open the Health tab and tap the settings button to choose data types. If Health has no data for today, or access is off in the Health app (Health > your profile > Apps > Daily Buddy), nothing can be shown.

I forgot my Daily Buddy passcode.

On the lock screen, tap "Forgot?" to unlock with Face ID, Touch ID or your iPhone passcode, then set a new code in Settings > App Lock.

My reminder didn't arrive.

Check iOS Settings > Notifications > Daily Buddy, and that no Focus is silencing it.

Where is my data? Can I move it to a new phone?

Everything stays on your iPhone and moves with an iPhone backup or transfer. You can also export PDF or Excel from Settings > Privacy & Data > Export Data.
